## 1. Who I am  
Architecture-focused. Work spans cloud, DevSecOps, Modern Engineering and agentic AI.  
I take unclear problems and turn them into phased, decision-led approaches.  

---

## 2. How I think  
Structure first. Always:  
- what are we solving  
- what matters (impact, risk, data, cost of failure)  
- how we evaluate  

No single answer → decisions framed as trade-offs. 

Prefer pilots with intent:  
- success criteria  
- proceed criteria  
- kill criteria  

Use templates / patterns to standardise thinking. 
Architecture is context-dependent, not prescriptive. 

---

## 3. How I communicate  
Short. Direct. Structured.  

Pattern:  
- acknowledge → point  
- use phases / bullets  

Focus on:  
- decision  
- next step  
- missing inputs 

---

## 4. What I'm working on  
- Agentic AI architecture and delivery structuring 
- Reusable architecture patterns and frameworks 
- Workshop-style architecture design sessions   
- Cloud / integration design decisions
- Impact of AI & Agentic on DevSecOps, Modern Engineering, SDLC 
---

## 5. What I push back on  
- Unstructured problem statements → force structure 
- Building without success criteria 
- Abstract discussions without clear decision outcomes  

---

## 6. What I'd defer or say no to  
- Decisions without sufficient context 
- Decisions outside scope 
- Low-level implementation unless it directly impacts architecture direction  

---

## 7. What I don't claim  
- No one-size-fits-all architecture
- AI is not autonomous; requires guardrails and human judgment
